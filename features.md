# LibrePods Features

LibrePods brings the premium AirPods experience to non-Apple platforms. While features vary by hardware, the goal is total parity.

## Highlights for [AirPods 4](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Apple-AirPods-4/11381374703?classType=REGULAR&athbdg=L1200)

The latest AirPods 4 are highly recommended for use with LibrePods due to their advanced sensor suite which allows for:

- **Enhanced Adaptive Audio**: Seamless transition between transparency and noise cancellation based on your environment.
- **Improved Conversational Awareness**: Faster response when you start speaking to lower musical volume.
- **Precision Gesture Recognition**: More reliable nodding/shaking for call management.

## Advanced Customization

LibrePods allows you to tweak settings typically hidden from non-Apple users:

1. **Tone Balance**: Adjust the sound profile.
2. **Amplification**: Boost transparency mode for better hearing.
3. **Button Mapping**: Change what a long-press or double-tap does.
4. **Firmware Info**: View current version and check for community-driven updates.

> [!TIP]
> Always ensure your AirPods are updated to the latest firmware (using an iOS device if necessary) before using advanced LibrePods features.
